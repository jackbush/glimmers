# Leaf: what's left

The roadmap is done: fruit, flower clusters, many cheap leaves, short shoots and side branches, cones, thorns, fan, cut and palm leaves (see NOTES.md). What the engine still can't draw:

- **Redwood's second foliage:** scale leaves pressed to its leading shoots, beside the flat needles on its side shoots. Needs two leaf kinds on one sprig.
- **Spruce's needles truly four-sided**, a hard ridge with a facet each side (Ridge only shades them).

## Trees Leaf can't draw

Tried and cut: they need something the engine draws a sprig of separate, pressed-flat leaves with can't give.

- **Monkey puzzle** (Araucaria araucana): its leaves overlap all round a thick branch so it reads as a scaly rope. Pressed flat at the sides of a stem, even dozens of small spiralled triangles read as a ragged fishbone, not a rope: it needs leaves drawn wrapping the stem, front and back, in a scale pattern over its whole width.
- **Lawson cypress** (Chamaecyparis lawsoniana), and Leyland: no leaves to speak of, just tiny scales clasping the shoot, so each shoot is a flat braided cord, branching again and again into a fern-like spray. Tiny pressed leaves read as a toothed twig, and one level of side shoots as a fishbone; it needs a shoot drawn as one braided strip with its scales as a pattern on it, and sprays branching two or three levels deep.
- Also tried before, and not themselves: sweet gum, silk tree, lilac, hornbeam (see NOTES.md, Species presets).
